A few things about this website

Privacy

About my site

My name is Samuele Laorni, and I made this Daft Punk website for a class project. I wanted to bring together their music, photos, and live shows in one place. This is not their official website.

Your information

I don’t collect your name, email, or payment details on this site. There are no accounts to make and no tracking tools added to the pages.

Links and shopping

Some links take you to other websites, like the official Daft Punk store. If you buy something, you do it on that store’s website. I don’t sell anything here or handle payments. Prices can change, so check the store before buying.

Photos and music

I found the photos and album covers online. I didn’t take the photos or make the music. They belong to the artists, photographers, and other people who created them. You can open the credits below to see where I found them.
