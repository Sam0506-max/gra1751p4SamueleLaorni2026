Daft Punk / Live archive

Live. Loud. Alive.

Two tours. Two different worlds. Revisit the club energy of 1997 and the pyramid shows of 2006–2007.

01 / The Homework era

Daftendirektour 1997

Touring behind Homework, Thomas Bangalter and Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo brought their early sound to the stage: drum machines, looping grooves, and a direct connection to the dance floor. The photograph captures the duo in silhouette during their Southampton show.

The period is preserved on Alive 1997, recorded at the Que Club in Birmingham and released in 2001.

02 / The pyramid era

Coachella 2006 → Alive 2007

The pyramid show debuted at Coachella in April 2006 before touring the world through 2007. Robot helmets, leather jackets, and geometric lights turned the duo’s music into a full stage spectacle, bringing tracks from Homework, Discovery, and Human After All together.

The live album Alive 2007 captures the June 14, 2007 performance at Bercy in Paris. The pictures above show Coachella in 2006 and the Berkeley stop in 2007.
