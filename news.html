Official announcement / October 22, 2026

Daft Punk × LEGO is official.

LEGO and Daft Punk have announced a partnership, and an official LEGO Ideas set inspired by the Alive 2007 pyramid stage is now in development.

The fan-designed Daft Punk Concert concept has been approved to become an official set. LEGO says more details are coming in 2027; a release date and price have not yet been announced.

Fortnite collaboration / September 2025

The Daft Punk Experience.

Daft Punk entered Fortnite with an interactive music experience that premiered on September 27, 2025. Fans could explore the duo’s world through music, digital spaces, and robot-inspired outfits.

The collaboration included remixing tracks in Dream Chamber Studios, battling robots in Robot Rock Arena, and dancing at the Daft Club, alongside Daft Punk cosmetics and LEGO styles.
